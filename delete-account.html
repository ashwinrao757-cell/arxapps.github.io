Delete Your Free Food Account

You can request deletion of your Free Food account and the personal data associated with it. You do not need to uninstall the app to make a request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to ashwinrao757@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line Free Food Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address used to sign in to Free Food. If available, include your in-app user ID.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

Email an account deletion request

What data will be deleted

After verifying your request, we will delete your account and personal data associated with it that we control, where applicable. This may include:

  • Your account and profile information.
  • Food posts and related photographs that you created.
  • Business or vendor profile information associated with your account.
  • Saved items, favourites and account preferences.
  • Other personal information associated with your account where deletion is technically and legally possible.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained when necessary to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, prevent fraud or maintain security. Where information must be retained, we will limit access to it and retain it only for as long as necessary for that purpose.

Messages or content already shared with other users may remain visible to those users if removing them would affect their records or the operation of the service. We will remove or de-identify personal information where reasonably possible.

Processing time

We will review and process verified deletion requests within 30 days. If any data must be retained, or if additional time is needed, we will explain the reason by email. Copies held in routine backups may persist temporarily until those backups are rotated or overwritten.

Delete some data without deleting your account

If you want to remove an individual food post or other specific information but keep your account, email ashwinrao757@gmail.com and describe what you want deleted. You may also use any deletion controls available within the app.
